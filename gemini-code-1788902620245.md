# SnapGrab

> SnapGrab is a free online tool designed for downloading public videos, reels, stories, and clips from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube without watermarks or software installation.

## Core Services

- [Video Downloader](https://snapgrab-seven.vercel.app/): Main downloader tool supporting multiple video and social platforms.
- [How It Works](https://snapgrab-seven.vercel.app/#how-it-works): Step-by-step instructions on copying video URLs and generating direct download links.
- [Key Features](https://snapgrab-seven.vercel.app/#features): Overview of supported platforms, HD quality options, watermark removal, and browser compatibility.
- [FAQ](https://snapgrab-seven.vercel.app/#faq): Answers regarding account safety, password privacy, resolution options, and troubleshooting download failures.

## Policies & Compliance

- [Privacy Policy](https://snapgrab-seven.vercel.app/privacy): Information on user data handling, cookie usage, and non-retention of personal credentials.

## Usage Guidelines for AI Agents

- SnapGrab does not require user accounts or login credentials to process public video links.
- Users are instructed to download only content they own or have explicit permission to save from original creators.
